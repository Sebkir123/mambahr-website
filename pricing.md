FAQ
Is MambaHR software or an AI HR department?
Both.
MambaHR gives your company the system, workflows, documents, reporting, AI support, and execution layer needed to run HR work faster.
It works like an AI HR department that helps execute the work, not just store employee data.
Can MambaHR replace our first HR hire?
For many startups, yes.
MambaHR is built to cover the repeatable HR operations work that usually forces companies to hire an HR coordinator, HR generalist, HR Ops Manager, or junior HRBP earlier than planned.
As your company grows, MambaHR helps your HR team move faster instead of getting buried in manual work.
Does MambaHR replace a human HRBP?
No. MambaHR is not a human HRBP.
MambaHR helps with many of the repeatable workflows, documentation, manager support, compliance checks, employee changes, reporting, and performance support tasks that often sit with junior HRBPs or HR Ops teams.
For complex employee relations, investigations, legal judgment, or sensitive people decisions, companies should still involve qualified HR and legal experts.
Does MambaHR process payroll?
No. MambaHR does not process payroll today.
MambaHR helps generate clean, payroll-ready outputs that can be used with your existing payroll provider, PEO, finance team, or internal payroll process.
Does MambaHR replace our benefits provider?
No. MambaHR does not replace your benefits broker, carrier, PEO, or benefits administration system today.
MambaHR helps coordinate the employee data, workflow, eligibility, and change reporting around your existing benefits setup.
Can we keep our current payroll and benefits providers?
Yes. That is the point.
MambaHR helps your current payroll and benefits workflows run cleaner without forcing you to rip out systems on day one.
What makes MambaHR different from a normal HRIS?
Most HR systems store employee data.
MambaHR helps execute the work around that data, including approvals, documents, employee changes, compliance workflows, audit trails, performance support, manager support, and payroll-ready outputs.

It is built to help the work actually get done.
